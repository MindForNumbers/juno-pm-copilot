# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno 6-Agent PRD Generator

_Module 6. Generated by `M6 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `06-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **Task / product:** Juno 6-Agent PRD Generator
- **Reviewer audience:** 2 Senior PMs + 1 Engineering Lead (for technical risk validation)
- **Value proposition:** Synthesize messy customer signals into evidence-backed PRD drafts evaluated by a 6-agent review squad, ensuring prioritization is grounded in $\ge$3 verbatim quotes and strategic pillars before Jira export.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | 1 (fail) | 3 (ok) | 5 (excellent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence Accuracy | Hallucinates quotes or bypasses the 3-quote gate | Quotes are real but lack sufficient context | Extracts $\ge$3 valid quotes, perfectly contextualized with precise source IDs |
| Strategic Alignment | Contradicts or hallucinates a strategic pillar | Maps to the correct pillar but justification is weak | Exact pillar mapping with deep justification and trade-off analysis |
| Adversarial Risk Flagging | Misses a glaring P0 risk (e.g., severe tech debt) | Flags risks correctly but descriptions are vague | Precise, actionable risk flags with zero false-positive noise |
| Jira Readiness | Output is poorly formatted or unusable | Acceptable Markdown, requires minor PM editing | Perfect Epic structure with clear, data-driven acceptance criteria |

_Full 1-5 anchors:_

### 1. Evidence Accuracy

- **Score 1:** Hallucinates customer quotes or extracts 0 verifiable quotes but bypasses the fail-safe gate.
- **Score 2:** Quotes are real but entirely irrelevant to the core problem being solved.
- **Score 3:** Extracts valid quotes but misses crucial context from the transcript.
- **Score 4:** Extracts $\ge$3 valid, highly relevant quotes accurately.
- **Score 5:** Extracts $\ge$3 valid quotes, perfectly contextualized, with precise traceability back to exact timestamps or ticket IDs.

### 2. Strategic Alignment

- **Score 1:** Maps the PRD to a completely wrong or non-existent strategic pillar.
- **Score 2:** Maps to a tangentially related pillar that doesn't fit the primary use case.
- **Score 3:** Right pillar identified, but the generated rationale is generic or weak.
- **Score 4:** Right pillar identified with strong, logical justification.
- **Score 5:** Right pillar identified with strong justification, explicitly highlighting specific strategic trade-offs based on the M3 Strategy document.

### 3. Adversarial Risk Flagging

- **Score 1:** The 6-agent squad misses a glaring P0 risk (e.g., severe tech debt, legal compliance) entirely.
- **Score 2:** Flags a risk but assigns the wrong severity or attributes it to the wrong persona (e.g., PMM flagging a backend database issue).
- **Score 3:** Flags correct risks, but the warning descriptions are vague and not immediately actionable.
- **Score 4:** Flags correct risks with clear, actionable warnings aligned to the correct agent personas.
- **Score 5:** Flags correct risks, provides highly specific mitigation steps, and perfectly suppresses noise (no false positives).

### 4. Jira Readiness

- **Score 1:** Unusable format, hallucinates Jira fields, or outputs a wall of unstructured text.
- **Score 2:** Poorly formatted, requires heavy manual editing by the PM to be usable.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable Markdown format, minor edits needed before pushing to Jira.
- **Score 4:** Clean Markdown, well-structured problem statement and business goals.
- **Score 5:** Perfect Jira Epic structure, with acceptance criteria explicitly defined and traceable directly to the customer data.

## Calibration

- **Sampling rule:** 50 PRD runs/week, stratified by agent conflict level (runs where agents strongly disagreed vs. agreed).
- **Cadence:** Weekly batch (every Friday afternoon).
- **Graders per item:** 2 Senior PMs + 1 Engineering Lead + Product Ops tiebreak.
- **Calibration cadence:** Re-calibrate quarterly, or immediately following an update to the Git Strategy One-Pager.

Differ by $\ge$2 on Evidence Accuracy or Risk Flagging => auto-escalate to Product Lead. Disagreement rate $\ge$15% on any dimension triggers a mandatory re-calibration session.

## Pass bar

$\ge$4.5/5 on Evidence Accuracy; 0 hallucinated quotes; 0 missed P0 tech debt risks.
